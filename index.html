About GSI

GSI stands for Get, Save, and Invest.

It is a personal financial ledger designed to help you track where every bit of your money went. Before taking out or spending any money, the system requires you to prove that the expense will yield a larger amount of money than the amount withdrawn.

Even for everyday essentials like food or clothing, you must specify the monetary amount you will earn because you ate that food or wore that cloth. If the projected yield is not bigger than the withdrawal, the system will not allow you to withdraw the money.

Later on, you can return to the ledger, tick if you achieved what you said the money was for, and fill in to what extent it was successful.

Note: Real money will not be deposited or withdrawn from your bank account; this is purely for personal accounting and discipline.
